Ask Siri for songsA key for a Shortcut on your iPhone. It can ask for music as you, and do nothing else, and you can take it back whenever you like.
  1. On your iPhone open Shortcuts, tap +, and add Dictate Text.
  2. Add Get Contents of URL and paste this address:
  3. Set Method to POST and Request Body to JSON.
  4. Under Headers add X-Music-Key with this value:
  5. Add a field: key input, type Text, value Dictated Text.
  6. Name it something you'd say out loud, like “Play music”. Then: “Hey Siri, Play music”.

Keep the key to yourself: anyone with it can ask for songs as you.
